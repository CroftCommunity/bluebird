Garden Saves

What you see

Skylite shows you posts from accounts your sponsor picked for you. It's a window to a small part of Bluesky — not the whole internet.

Why it feels calm

There are no likes, no follower counts, and no comments. Nothing here is trying to keep you scrolling. It's made to be a quiet window, like looking up at the stars.

What your sponsor can do

They choose the channels you see, they can turn channels on and off, and they can pause Skylite. When it's paused, the garden waits until they turn it back on.

What's public and what's private

The posts you see are public on Bluesky — other people can see them too. Your Saves and the notes you write are private. They stay only on this device and no one else can see them.

If something feels wrong

It's always okay to ask for help. You can reach an adult you trust any time.
